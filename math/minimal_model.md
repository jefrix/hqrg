“A Minimal Calculable Model in Holonomic Quantum Reality: Corrections to the Hidden-Order Transition in URu₂Si₂ (Path A)”
Or more generally:
“First Quantitative Signature from the Path A Framework: Application to URu₂Si₂”

Required Sections (Minimal Viable Structure)
1. Scope and Framing (Critical – Addresses Equivocation)

Explicitly state that this document works strictly within Path A.
Clearly define the status of ρ^ent: a fundamental phenomenological scalar field, not derived from von Neumann entanglement entropy.
State the goal narrowly: to extract one concrete, calculable correction to an observable in a real physical system.
Declare what the document does not claim (e.g., it does not derive geometry from entanglement, nor does it constitute a full unification).

2. Metric Signature and Lagrangian

Explicitly declare the metric signature (recommend mostly-minus (+,−,−,−) for consistency with most particle physics and condensed matter literature).
Write the full 11D Lagrangian with consistent signs for the kinetic and potential terms of ρ^ent.
Briefly restate the form of $  \mathcal{L}_{\rm hidden}  $ (topological sector).

3. Compactification Ansatz (Addresses the Projection Problem)
This is one of the most important sections.

Choose and explicitly specify one structured compactification (do not use flat $  T^7  $).
Recommended starting point: A warped product on $  T^7  $ or a simple flux compactification.
Clearly write the 11D metric ansatz:$$ds_{11}^2 = e^{2A(y)} g_{\mu\nu}(x) dx^\mu dx^\nu + g_{mn}(y) dy^m dy^n$$

Specify the warp factor form (even if phenomenological, e.g., depending on one internal coordinate).
State what is integrated over and what approximations are made (zero modes only, etc.).
Define the projection operator $  \Delta^{\rm proj}_{\mu\nu}  $ explicitly for this ansatz (even if approximate).

4. Reduction of the Informational Sector

Perform the dimensional reduction of the ρ^ent sector (kinetic + non-minimal + potential terms) under the chosen ansatz.
Derive the effective 4D contributions to the stress-energy tensor coming from ρ^ent.
Show the leading correction terms that survive after integration over the internal space.

5. Coupling to the Target System (URu₂Si₂)

Introduce a minimal, explicit coupling between the reduced informational sector and the hidden-order parameter ψ of URu₂Si₂.
Example: Effective terms in the free energy such as $  \lambda \rho_{\rm eff} |\psi|^2  $ or derivative couplings.

Justify the form of the coupling (phenomenological but motivated).
Write the effective 4D Landau free energy that includes both the conventional hidden-order terms and the new informational contributions.

6. Derivation of the Observable

Derive (even approximately) the shift in the transition temperature:$$T_{\rm HO}^{\rm eff} = T_{\rm HO}^{(0)} + \delta T(\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \lambda, \dots)$$
Or derive the modification to the specific heat jump / entropy release.
Show the key steps — do not just state the final result.

7. Parameter Estimation and Bounds

Use existing experimental data on URu₂Si₂ (specific heat jump, entropy release, transition width) to place bounds on the new coupling constants.
Estimate the expected size of $  \delta T_{\rm HO}  $ (or other observables) for allowed parameter ranges.
This section is crucial for addressing the “no numeric prediction” objection.

8. Prediction and Falsifiability

State the concrete prediction clearly (e.g., “Within the allowed parameter range, we expect a shift of order X–Y mK” or “a modification to the specific heat jump of Z%”).
Discuss how this could be tested (or is already constrained) by existing or near-future experiments.
Compare with conventional explanations (e.g., multipolar order, density waves) — what would be distinctive about an informational correction?

9. Limitations and Scope

Explicitly list what was assumed or approximated.
State what would be required to make the prediction more robust (better compactification, microscopic derivation of the coupling, etc.).
Reiterate that this is a first calculable signature within Path A, not a complete theory.

10. Summary and Outlook (Short)

One-paragraph summary of what was achieved.
Clear statement of next technical steps.
