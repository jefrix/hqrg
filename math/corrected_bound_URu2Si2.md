# Corrected Bound on \(\delta T_{\rm HO}\): URu₂Si₂ Sensitivity vs. Fifth-Force Suppression

**Purpose:** provide a short, self-contained derivation suitable for inclusion in the HQR manifesto, website, or model notes.

---

## 1. The Landau Shift

Start with the phenomenological free energy

\[
F_{\rm eff}(\eta)
=
\frac{1}{2}a\left(T-T_{\rm HO}^{(0)}\right)\eta^2
+
\frac{u}{4}\eta^4
+
\lambda_{\phi\psi}\langle\phi\rangle\eta^2,
\]

where

\[
\eta = |\psi|
\]

is the hidden-order amplitude and \(\phi\) is the effective Path A informational scalar.

The transition occurs when the effective quadratic coefficient changes sign. Therefore

\[
\delta T_{\rm HO}
=
-\frac{\lambda_{\phi\psi}\langle\phi\rangle}{a}.
\]

The directly constrained quantity is

\[
\frac{\lambda_{\phi\psi}\langle\phi\rangle}{a}.
\]

This is why the problem must be split into two different questions:

1. What level of \(\delta T_{\rm HO}\) could URu₂Si₂ thermodynamics plausibly detect or bound?
2. What level of \(\delta T_{\rm HO}\) is allowed if \(\phi\) is an ordinary unscreened long-range scalar constrained by fifth-force experiments?

These are not the same bound.

---

## 2. Phenomenological URu₂Si₂ Bound

URu₂Si₂ has a hidden-order transition near

\[
T_{\rm HO}\approx 17.5\,{\rm K}.
\]

Existing thermodynamic measurements constrain unmodeled shifts in the transition through sample stability, transition width, pressure/strain sensitivity, and measurement precision.

A useful target scale is

\[
|\delta T_{\rm HO}|_{\rm phen}
\lesssim
10\text{--}50\,{\rm mK}.
\]

This is a **phenomenological material sensitivity scale**.

It is not a fifth-force bound.

A more conservative sample-variation bound is

\[
|\delta T_{\rm HO}|_{\rm sample}
\lesssim
\mathcal{O}(1)\,{\rm K}.
\]

This is safer but less useful.

---

## 3. Why Fifth-Force Constraints Do Not Directly Apply

Fifth-force experiments constrain scalar couplings to ordinary matter. The HQR Landau shift depends on a hidden-sector coupling:

\[
\lambda_{\phi\psi}.
\]

Unless HQR supplies a matching relation between \(\lambda_{\phi\psi}\) and ordinary matter coupling, fifth-force tests do not directly bound

\[
\lambda_{\phi\psi}\langle\phi\rangle.
\]

Therefore the statement

\[
|\delta T_{\rm HO}|\lesssim50\,{\rm mK}
\]

cannot honestly be described as a fifth-force-derived result.

It is a phenomenological sensitivity target.

---

## 4. Bound Under Universal Unscreened Scalar Coupling

If, and only if, \(\phi\) behaves as an ordinary unscreened long-range scalar whose hidden-order coupling tracks ordinary matter coupling, then fifth-force limits become extremely restrictive.

The scalar-induced fractional shift scales as

\[
\frac{\delta T_{\rm HO}}{T_{\rm HO}}
\lesssim
\alpha_\phi\frac{\Phi_\oplus}{c^2}.
\]

Using

\[
\frac{\Phi_\oplus}{c^2}
\approx
7\times10^{-10},
\]

and a MICROSCOPE-level long-range scalar bound

\[
\alpha_\phi\lesssim10^{-11},
\]

we obtain

\[
\frac{\delta T_{\rm HO}}{T_{\rm HO}}
\lesssim
7\times10^{-21}.
\]

Since

\[
T_{\rm HO}\approx17.5\,{\rm K},
\]

this gives

\[
|\delta T_{\rm HO}|_{\rm fifth-force}
\lesssim
1.2\times10^{-19}\,{\rm K}.
\]

Even the looser direct equivalence-principle scale

\[
\eta\sim2.7\times10^{-15}
\]

would imply

\[
|\delta T_{\rm HO}|
\lesssim
4.7\times10^{-14}\,{\rm K}.
\]

Both are far below millikelvin detectability.

---

## 5. Physical Implication for HQR

A detectable mK-scale transition shift in URu₂Si₂ cannot come from a standard unscreened universal fifth-force scalar.

Therefore, if HQR predicts an observable effect in URu₂Si₂, it must involve one or more non-generic mechanisms:

1. **Screening:** the scalar is hidden from equivalence-principle tests but active inside the correlated-electron environment.
2. **Material-sector-specific coupling:** \(\lambda_{\phi\psi}\) is not tied to baryon/mass coupling.
3. **Topological coupling:** the hidden-order sector couples through protected higher-dimensional/topological terms rather than ordinary scalar charge.
4. **Correlated observables:** the signature appears through a pattern in pressure, strain, magnetic field, entropy release, susceptibility, or neutron response, rather than a simple absolute shift in \(T_{\rm HO}\).

This is a stronger position than claiming a simple \(50\,{\rm mK}\) fifth-force-compatible signal.

It makes the HQR prediction more distinctive and more falsifiable.
