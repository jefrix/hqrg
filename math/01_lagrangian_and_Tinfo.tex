\begin{abstract}
We present the 11-dimensional Lagrangian of Holonomic Quantum Reality under the Path A commitment, in which \(\rho^{\rm ent}\) is treated as a fundamental phenomenological scalar field. We derive the quantum informational stress-energy tensor \(T^{\rm info}_{AB}\) and demonstrate explicit dimensional reduction on a flat 7-torus.
\end{abstract}

\section{Path A Commitment}

In this formulation, \(\rho^{\rm ent}\) is a fundamental scalar field on the 11D manifold. The name ``entanglement density'' is interpretive and does not imply that \(\rho^{\rm ent}\) is derived from the von Neumann entropy of a reduced density matrix.

\section{The 11D Lagrangian}

The Lagrangian density is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\rm HQR} = \frac{R^{(11)}}{16\pi G_{11}} 
+ \alpha \, \rho \, R^{(11)} 
+ \frac{\beta}{2} g^{AB} \nabla_A \rho \, \nabla_B \rho 
+ \gamma \, V(\rho) 
+ \mathcal{L}_{\rm hidden},
\end{equation}
where \(\rho = \rho^{\rm ent}\).

The hidden-order sector includes topological contributions:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\rm hidden} = \frac{\kappa}{3!} C \wedge dC \wedge dC 
+ \lambda \, \rho \, \operatorname{Tr}(R \wedge R \wedge R \wedge R \wedge R)
+ \theta(\rho) \, \operatorname{CS}_5(F).
\end{equation}

\section{Derivation of \( T^{\rm info}_{AB} \)}

Define \( f = \frac{1}{16\pi G_{11}} + \alpha \rho \). Variation of the action with respect to the metric yields the modified Einstein equation
\begin{equation}
f \big( R_{AB} - \tfrac12 R g_{AB} \big) - \big( \nabla_A \nabla_B - g_{AB} \square \big)(\alpha \rho) 
= 8\pi G_{11} \, T_{AB}^{\rm (kin+pot + matter + hidden)}.
\end{equation}

Rearrangement into standard form gives the effective informational stress-energy tensor
\begin{equation}
T_{AB}^{\rm info} = \frac{1}{f} \Bigg[ 
\beta \Big( \nabla_A \rho \, \nabla_B \rho - \tfrac12 g_{AB} |\nabla\rho|^2 \Big) 
+ g_{AB} \, \gamma V(\rho) 
+ \frac{\alpha}{8\pi G_{11}} \big( \nabla_A \nabla_B \rho - g_{AB} \square \rho \big) 
\Bigg].
\end{equation}

\section{Explicit Reduction on Flat \( T^7 \)}

Consider the product metric ansatz
\[
ds_{11}^2 = g_{\mu\nu}(x)\, dx^\mu dx^\nu + \delta_{mn}\, dy^m dy^n
\]
on \( M^4 \times T^7 \), with \(\rho = \rho_0(x)\) independent of the internal coordinates.

The internal derivatives vanish, and the reduction of the non-minimal contribution to the effective 4D energy density is
\[
T_{00}^{(4)}(x) = C \big( \nabla_0 \nabla_0 \rho_0 - g_{00} \square_4 \rho_0 \big),
\]
where \( C \) is a constant set by \(\alpha\) and \( G_{11}\). The volume factors cancel exactly, yielding a finite, definite 4D expression.

This demonstrates that the reduction procedure is well-defined and calculable once the compactification ansatz and profile of \(\rho\) are specified.

\section{Notes}

\begin{itemize}
    \item The torus example serves as a minimal consistency check. More realistic compactifications (warped products, fluxes, \(G_2\) or Calabi-Yau manifolds) follow the same structural procedure but involve additional technical steps.
    \item Topological terms in \(\mathcal{L}_{\rm hidden}\) primarily affect the dynamics of \(\rho\) and global selection rules rather than contributing directly to the local stress-energy tensor.
\end{itemize}
